Research direction

Listen before acting.

Waveform Resonance Mechanics is an experimental framework for reasoning about change, context, trust, recovery, and restraint in distributed intelligent systems.

Preserve evidence. Treat uncertainty as information. Keep human judgment visible.

Design principles

  1. Evidence should precede automation.
  2. Human judgment should remain visible.
  3. Recovery is part of intelligence.
  4. Explainability is an operational feature.
  5. Local-first is preferable when practical.
  6. Restraint can be a sign of competence.
